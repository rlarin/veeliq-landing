What is VeelIQ? +

VeelIQ is an AI solutions provider built on MCP. Today we ship two solutions: Context, a private knowledge and memory layer for your AI (in Beta with a free plan), and the Skills Marketplace, a subscription catalog of expert skills (coming soon). Both plug into the AI you already use through one URL.

What is Context? +

Context is a private context layer for your AI. Upload your documents and your agent answers from them; ask it to remember something and it recalls it next session, in any client. It is live in Beta with a free plan.

When does the Skills Marketplace launch? +

Coming soon. The waitlist is open, and Context subscribers will see it first.

Do I need an API key or AI model? +

No. VeelIQ rides on top of the AI you already use — Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT. You never provide a model key. We only act as a tool provider over MCP.

Which clients are supported? +

Any MCP-compatible client. That includes Claude.ai, Cursor, ChatGPT, Zed, Windsurf, and growing. One URL covers all of them.

Is my data private? +

Yes. Your data lives in your own isolated space, enforced with row level security. We never retain the content of your AI conversations; tool calls are logged with metadata only (tool, timestamp, latency) for reliability and billing.
