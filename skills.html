06 · FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Still curious? Our docs go deeper — or ping us at app.veeliq.com.

What is the Skills Marketplace? +

The Skills Marketplace is one of VeelIQ's AI solutions: a subscription marketplace for AI skills. Each skill is a curated package of prompts, retrieval, and guardrails, packaged as an MCP tool. Subscribe, paste the URL into your AI client, and the skills appear as native tools. It is coming soon; join the waitlist to get in first.

Do I need an API key or AI model? +

No. VeelIQ rides on top of the AI you already use — Claude.ai, Cursor, ChatGPT. You never provide a model key. We only act as a tool provider over MCP.

Which clients are supported? +

Any MCP-compatible client. That includes Claude.ai, Cursor, Zed, Windsurf, and growing. One URL covers all of them.

Can I build a skill and earn on it? +

Yes — the Creator Program is in beta. Write a 20-line YAML manifest, publish, and earn 70% of invocation revenue.

How is pricing structured? +

Skills are priced individually (most $5–$20/mo). Bundle plans cover popular combos. There's a free tier with starter skills to try it out.

Who can see my data? +

VeelIQ never retains the content of your AI conversations. Tool calls are logged with metadata only (skill, timestamp, latency) for reliability and billing.
