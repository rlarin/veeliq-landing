Comparison

VeelIQ vs NotebookLM.

Both let an AI answer from your documents. The architectural difference — destination app vs portable layer — decides which one fits your workflow.

NotebookLM is Google's AI research assistant: you upload sources into a notebook and interact with them inside Google's interface, with standout features like Audio Overviews. VeelIQ Context inverts the model: your sources live behind an MCP endpoint, and the AI clients you already use — Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor — query them where you already work. NotebookLM is a place you go; VeelIQ is a capability your existing tools gain.

Strengths of NotebookLM

  • Polished, purpose-built research interface with zero setup.
  • Audio Overviews turn sources into listenable summaries — genuinely unique.
  • Handles diverse source types, including YouTube videos and audio.
  • Free and backed by Google's infrastructure.

Strengths of VeelIQ

  • Client-agnostic: the same corpus serves Claude, ChatGPT, and your IDE — you keep your preferred model and interface.
  • Adds persistent memory, which NotebookLM does not have at all.
  • Context follows you into agentic workflows (coding agents, scheduled agents) where a web app cannot go.
  • Per-user isolation with row-level security and no training on your content.

Limitations to know

  • VeelIQ has no audio/video features and requires an MCP-capable client; it is also younger (Beta).
  • NotebookLM keeps your work inside one Google product; sources and chats don't follow you to other AI tools, and there is no cross-session memory of you.

Which should you choose?

Choose NotebookLM if you want a self-contained research app for a bounded project — especially if Audio Overviews appeal to you. Choose VeelIQ if you live in multiple AI tools and want one knowledge base plus memory that works in all of them. Plenty of people use both: NotebookLM for deep-dive sessions, VeelIQ as the always-on context layer.
