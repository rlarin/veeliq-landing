01

Who We Are

VeelIQ ("VeelIQ", "we", "our", or "us") is the company operating this website and the VeelIQ platform — a Skills-as-a-Service product that provides plug-and-play AI skills for individuals and businesses via the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle the personal data we collect through this website and our platform.

02

Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the service. This includes:

  • Email address — provided voluntarily when you sign up or create an account.
  • Referrer URL — the page you visited before arriving on our site, captured automatically by your browser.
  • User agent string — your browser and operating system, collected to understand our audience.
  • Sign-up timestamp — the date and time your submission was recorded.
  • Subscription and billing data — managed by our payment processor; we do not store full card details.
We do not collect your name, phone number, or any sensitive personal data beyond what is listed above. We do not use tracking pixels or third-party advertising networks.

03

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your VeelIQ account and subscription.
  • To deliver the skills and tools you have subscribed to via your MCP server URL.
  • To send transactional emails (account confirmation, billing receipts, subscription changes).
  • To understand where our audience is coming from and improve the product experience.
  • To prevent fraud and maintain the integrity of the service.

We will never sell your data or use it for purposes incompatible with those listed above.

04

Data Storage & Security

Account data is stored in Supabase, a managed PostgreSQL platform with industry-standard security practices, including encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+).

Supabase infrastructure is hosted on AWS and is SOC 2 Type II compliant. Data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt) region.

While we take reasonable precautions to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

05

Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Supabase — our database provider (see Section 4).
  • Payment processor — billing data is handled by our third-party payment provider; we never store card numbers.
  • Google Fonts — font assets loaded from fonts.googleapis.com. Google may log the request; see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Legal authorities — if required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

All third-party providers are bound to process your data only as directed by us and in accordance with applicable data-protection laws.

06

Cookies & Tracking

This website currently does not use cookies, local storage, analytics scripts, or any persistent client-side tracking technologies beyond what is necessary for authentication.

Should we introduce analytics or marketing cookies in the future, this policy will be updated and appropriate consent mechanisms will be implemented before any tracking begins.

07

Data Retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. If you cancel your subscription, we retain minimal data for billing and legal compliance purposes for up to 3 years as required by applicable law.

Upon your request for deletion, your personal data will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days, subject to legal retention requirements.

08

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EU/EEA under GDPR, the UK under UK GDPR, and California under CCPA), you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction — request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — opt out of communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@veeliq.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

09

Children's Privacy

VeelIQ is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If changes are material, we will notify account holders by email.

Continued use of VeelIQ after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
